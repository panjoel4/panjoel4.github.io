Quantitative descriptors

How these values are calculated

Mean and median intensity use grayscale pixels inside the circular ROI. Bright-area fraction uses the selected threshold. Hotspots are connected bright components after small-noise filtering. Heterogeneity is the coefficient of variation. Entropy uses the grayscale histogram. Radial uniformity compares average intensity across concentric rings.

Capture guidance
