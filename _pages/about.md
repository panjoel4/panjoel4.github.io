Data geoscientist with 10+ years of experience in subsurface analysis, petrophysics, and exploration workflows. Currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Geology, applying artificial intelligence and hyperspectral data to advance digital subsurface characterization. Strong background in consultancy, R&D collaboration, and production-ready geoscience applications.

## Research interests

- Artificial intelligence for geoscience
- Hyperspectral imaging and mineral mapping
- Digital core and subsurface characterization
- Petrophysics and reservoir analysis
- Remote sensing for geological applications
- Python-based geoscience workflows
- Geothermal and unconventional reservoir studies

## Selected expertise

- Subsurface and petrophysical interpretation
- Machine learning and deep learning for geoscience
- Hyperspectral and multispectral data analysis
- Geological data integration and visualization
- Python, data science, and geospatial analytics
- Research, technical consulting, and workflow automation

## Current focus

I am developing AI-driven methods to extract geological meaning from complex subsurface datasets, especially hyperspectral core imagery and integrated reservoir data. My goal is to build practical, interpretable, and scalable tools that help geoscientists move from raw data to reliable subsurface insight.

## Research Groups & Collaborations

I am actively involved in collaborative research groups that connect geoscience, digital innovation, and applied artificial intelligence.

### Geovartha Research Group

I am part of the **Geovartha Research Group**, a collaborative geoscience initiative that connects applied research, technical consulting, and digital innovation in Earth science.

Through Geovartha, I contribute to projects involving subsurface evaluation, geospatial analysis, geothermal studies, remote sensing, and machine learning applications for geological interpretation. The group reflects my broader commitment to developing practical, data-driven workflows for exploration, resource evaluation, environmental studies, and sustainable subsurface development.
