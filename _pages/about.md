Data geoscientist with 9+ years of experience in subsurface analysis, petrophysics, and exploration workflows. Currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Geology, applying artificial intelligence and hyperspectral data to advance digital subsurface characterization. Strong background in consultancy, R&D collaboration, and scientific software development, with experience translating geoscientific workflows into Python-based desktop and web applications, analytical tools, and reproducible computational workflows.

- Digital core and subsurface characterization
- Scientific software development and reproducible research
- Petrophysics and reservoir analysis
- Remote sensing for geological applications

## Selected expertise

- Machine learning and deep learning for geoscience

## Current focus

My current work focuses on integrating geological interpretation, machine learning, and scientific software development to improve subsurface characterization. I am particularly interested in developing practical computational workflows and software applications for processing, analyzing, and visualizing complex geoscientific datasets. Through my research and software projects, I aim to make advanced analytical methods more accessible, reproducible, and useful for geoscientists in research and industry.

### PREDICT Research Group

The PREDICT Lab at KFUPM studies how carbonate systems form, evolve, and function as reservoirs. It combines sedimentology, diagenesis, geochemistry, remote sensing, hyperspectral imaging, and AI to interpret geoscience data across scales.
