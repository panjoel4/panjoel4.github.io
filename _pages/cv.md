Profile
======

Data geoscientist with 9+ years of experience in subsurface analysis, petrophysics, and exploration workflows. Currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Geology, applying artificial intelligence and hyperspectral data to advance digital subsurface characterization. Strong background in consultancy, R&D collaboration, and geoscience software development.

Core competencies include subsurface interpretation, subsurface data management, Python automation, AI for geoscience, spectral geology, and web applications for geoscience.
